# Network Security Fundamentals: term syllabus

1. The CIA triad (45 min) | keywords: confidentiality, integrity, availability
2. Threats, vulnerabilities and risk (45 min) | keywords: threat, vulnerability, exploit, risk, likelihood
3. How networks work: TCP/IP and ports (90 min) | keywords: packet, IP address, TCP, UDP, port
4. Firewalls and network segmentation (90 min) | keywords: firewall, rule, default deny, stateful, segmentation, DMZ
5. Passwords, hashing and multi-factor authentication (90 min) | keywords: password, hash, salt, MFA
6. Phishing and social engineering (45 min) | keywords: phishing, social engineering, pretexting, spear phishing
7. Malware and how to defend against it (45 min) | keywords: malware, virus, worm, ransomware, trojan
8. Encryption: symmetric and asymmetric (90 min) | keywords: encryption, symmetric, asymmetric, AES, RSA, public key
9. TLS and certificates (45 min) | keywords: HTTPS, TLS, certificate, certificate authority
10. Logging and incident response (45 min) | keywords: log, SIEM, incident, containment, recovery
